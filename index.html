    How it works

    The Opportunity Index, explained

    Transparent by design. Every score comes from five weighted factors, adjusted by each country's real business seasonality.

    🌍

    Market size

    Population, economic activity and the number of businesses that hire and train sellers.

    💳

    Purchasing power

    Ability and willingness to invest in professional education and tutoring.

    📶

    E-learning adoption

    Digital maturity and how comfortable the market is buying courses online.

    🤝

    Sales culture

    How central sales, commissions and negotiation are to the local economy.

    📅

    Seasonality

    Fiscal year-ends, holidays and hemisphere seasons that move demand each month.

    Note: figures are a curated, editable model for educational purposes — not live financial data.
